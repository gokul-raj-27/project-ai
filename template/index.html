The pipeline

Three steps, all in the browser

The same approach behind the demo below scales to a full sign vocabulary.

01 · Capture

Landmark detection

MediaPipe Hands tracks 21 key points per hand from the camera feed, 30+ times a second, entirely on-device.

02 · Classify

Lightweight classifier

Landmark positions and angles feed a small, fast model trained to recognize distinct hand shapes and gestures.

03 · Translate

Text in real time

Recognized signs are assembled into words and phrases, streamed to the screen as you sign.
